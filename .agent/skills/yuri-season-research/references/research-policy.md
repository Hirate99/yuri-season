# Research lane policy

Read the sections relevant to the leased task. The CLI's structured completion policy is authoritative for execution completeness; this document governs evidence and content judgment.

## Editorial operating judgment

- Coverage and publication are separate. Inspect and remember every in-scope original, but publish only material that gives readers new, attributable value.
- Work horizontally before going deep: cover every current work's official surface, then follow creators, main cast, members, media, and community leads according to evidence and current relevance.
- Cluster posts about the same event. Merge preview/start/in-progress reminders into one reader-facing update unless a correction, result, new visual, or material firsthand comment adds independent value.
- Prefer concrete changes, new visuals, schedules, releases, events, production insight, and firsthand creator/cast context over repetitive promotion. Record ignored originals with a reason instead of silently dropping them.
- Follow credible first-party links and newly verified terms proactively. A new account, tag, event page, creator credit, channel, or project nickname should become durable evidence or a follow-up task rather than a note that a person must rediscover later.
- After import, read the public projection as a reader would: Chinese title and summary, original text and translation when allowed, correct image, attribution, source link, related work, event timing, and duplicate behavior. Repair supported missing projections before declaring completion.
- Keep a per-work outcome in the cycle handoff: published/held items, important ignored clusters, new leads, blocked surfaces, and remaining freshness gaps. Do not notify on ordinary verified zero change.

## Official accounts and tags

- Inspect verified current-season work/project accounts from their actual timeline, platform API, or an explicitly allowed public embed. Do not substitute a title search for an account timeline.
- Timeline and tag surfaces are complementary. Share durable stable post IDs so the same original is not reconsidered through multiple paths.
- Recover active official work, anime, project, campaign, unit, and character tags from verified profiles and recent official originals. Preserve the term and its source post as evidence; do not hard-code a season's tags into the skill.
- A public embed can support account-timeline coverage when it reaches the previous cursor. It cannot prove exhaustive global newest-first tag coverage.
- Third-party metadata adapters may only enrich media URLs, dimensions, or quoted-original relationships after the canonical original has been recovered. They are never provenance.
- One original may support a feed candidate, event, schedule change, media item, account lead, or other structured projection. Reconcile every supported output.

## Cast, creators, and project members

Model cast content as `character → cast credit → person → verified account → work-specific original post`.

- Routine identity coverage is limited to original authors/artists and the recurring protagonist cast. Supporting or episode-only cast requires an explicit identity audit or a concrete work-related lead.
- Account discovery and verification are separate. An unverified account must create a follow-up verification task instead of being treated as permanently covered. First-party cross-links can supply verification evidence; ambiguous identity remains held for review.
- Ordinary cast/staff posts must explicitly mention the work, character, episode, or relevant event.
- Verified main-group 2.5D members/personas may also contribute public professional or creative activity tied to the project identity: performances, releases, appearances, interviews, rehearsals, behind-the-scenes work, streams, or credited output. Exclude private routine, context-free selfies, unrelated advertising/giveaways, and repost-only items without material firsthand text.
- Keep cast posts in their own deduplication lane: anime + account + stable platform object ID.

## Media and fanwork

- Use the original creator post, never an aggregator, mirror, search thumbnail, quote-post, or repost.
- Fanwork discovery searches Pixiv, X, and Instagram separately. Fanwork remains community, link-only, and held in phase one without affirmative reuse permission.
- AI-generated fanwork is out of scope. On Pixiv require the platform AI status to be explicitly non-AI; do not infer undocumented enum meanings or human authorship from style or missing tags. Explicit AI labels, disclosures, or generation-tool tags are rejected and remembered without creating a batch candidate.
- Apply a quality gate before review. Ordinary fanwork needs at least 100 publicly visible platform-native positive engagements (likes, favorites, or bookmarks in the platform's own terms) plus a completed, legible, non-placeholder presentation. Below-threshold or visibly low-effort work is `ignored`, not held, and never consumes the review queue.
- Give a post younger than 48 hours one maturity recheck instead of sending it to review below the threshold. Verified original authors/staff, an official campaign selection or award, and an explicit user-requested lead may bypass the engagement floor, but never the originality, AI, safety, or attribution checks. Do not add per-title thresholds.
- When an original exposes a real image or official preview, create linked media as well as the feed candidate. Preserve the original post as `media.originalUrl`, creator attribution, upstream URL/hash in internal evidence, safety, spoilers, and correction/takedown path.
- Never publish a placeholder, `NOW PRINTING` asset, unverified thumbnail, or media whose source prohibits reproduction/embedding. Use link-only when reuse is not supported.

## Characters, names, birthdays, episodes, and music

- Main-character coverage means the recurring protagonist group, not every character or cast entry. Record the expected group count, first-party baseline URL, and check time. Supporting records remain editable but outside the public main-group completeness score.
- Use the official native name as identity. Moegirl may provide Chinese display-name provenance only after an explicit one-to-one match; it cannot verify cast, birthdays, accounts, profiles, or group membership.
- Character birthdays require first-party profiles, calendars, publishers, or equivalent official material. Birthdays update character records and recurring calendar events, never a birthday feed candidate. Celebration content uses its normal official-art, creator-art, or cast-post lane.
- A birthday audit is explicit. Verified dates retain the exact source; a completed first-party zero-hit audit becomes durable `exhausted` memory with `rerunOn: first_party_source_change`. Never substitute a real ship's launch date or a search summary.
- Episode progress uses deterministic broadcast estimation until a released episode is confirmed by an official STORY, broadcaster, or distribution page. A preview alone does not advance verified progress.
- Theme-song identity/classification requires first-party evidence. Apple Music may supply an exact title-and-artist public action and artwork, but cannot independently prove song identity or OP/ED classification. Conflicts stop automatic writes.

## Community

- Store only the original thread title, canonical URL, platform, activity evidence, and entity relationship. Do not copy bodies or attachments.
- Query database discussions and durable hits before browsing. A cross-work thread is one canonical candidate with one anchor `animeId` and all materially covered `animeIds`.
- For 百合会, scan the signed-in animation-board recent list and match current-season titles, aliases, project names, characters, units, and recognizable pair names. Dedicated threads and materially active topical discussions are valid; generic chatter and one-line reactions are not.
- Treat 萌战吧 separately from a work's own Tieba. Preserve the platform exactly and reject bait, memes, snippets, and unrelated popularity contests.
- A strong opinion, negative review, or polarizing tone is not by itself a reason to hold an otherwise safe, clearly related, materially active original discussion. Publish the canonical link and activity metadata when the automatic boundary passes; moderation may withdraw or correct it afterward.

## Automatic publication boundary

Automatic publication requires verified provenance, direct entailment, safe content, no major spoiler, supported presentation, complete attribution, no identity conflict, and the category's confidence threshold. Community threads that meet this boundary publish without prior human review even when their opinion is controversial; reserve holds for major spoilers, safety issues, ambiguous work association, source conflicts, or other failures of the boundary. Moderation remains reversible after publication. Reject irrelevant, duplicate, contradicted, unsafe, inaccessible, or provenance-free material.
